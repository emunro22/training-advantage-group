Check Your Driver CPC Hours

What is Driver CPC?

Professional drivers of lorries, buses and coaches must complete 35 hours of periodic training every 5 years to keep their Driver Certificate of Professional Competence (CPC).

Use the official DVSA service to check your training hours and see when your next training is due. You'll need your driving licence number.

Check My CPC Hours →

Reminder: Training must be completed before your deadline. We offer flexible Driver CPC courses including evening and weekend options.
