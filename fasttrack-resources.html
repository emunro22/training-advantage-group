About Us

    FASTTRACK Resources is an external service division within the Training Advantage Group Ltd, supplying experienced professionals across the training and transport sectors.

    We provide qualified and approved personnel including:

  • Instructors and Trainers
  • Driving Assessors
  • Dangerous Goods Safety Advisers (DGSA)
  • External Transport Managers (ETM Services)

    • Whether you require temporary cover, ongoing support, or specialist consultancy, FASTTRACK Resources offers flexible, reliable staffing solutions tailored to your operational needs.

    All our professionals are fully vetted and competent in their field, ensuring you receive consistent, high-quality service in line with current legislation and industry best practice.
