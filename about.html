About Training Advantage Group

Scotland's Leading Nationwide Transport, Logistics and Construction Consultancy and Training Provider

Who We Are

Training Advantage Group Ltd is a comprehensive consultancy and training provider specialising in the transport, logistics, and construction sectors. We deliver tailored solutions from initial assessment to ongoing support, working with everyone from small van operators to large fleets across Scotland and beyond. Our mission is to ensure compliance, efficiency, and safety through quality training and expert consultancy services.

Our Divisions

FASTTRACK Training UK

FASTTRACK Training bridges the gap between learning and employment. We specialise in CV writing, job and interview preparation, and creating tailored personal development plans. Through our strong partnerships with recruitment and employment agencies, we offer practical work experience, supported employment placements, and opportunities for paid contracts. We don't just prepare you for the job — we help you get it.

FASTTRACK Resources

An external service division supplying experienced professionals across the training and transport sectors. We provide qualified and approved personnel including instructors and trainers, driving assessors, Dangerous Goods Safety Advisers (DGSA), and External Transport Managers (ETM Services). Whether you require temporary cover, ongoing support, or specialist consultancy, we offer flexible, reliable staffing solutions tailored to your operational needs.

FASTTRACK Cleaning & Maintenance

A specialist division delivering reliable and high-standard industrial and commercial cleaning services across Scotland and beyond. Our services include high-reach window cleaning, deep cleans for industrial premises, and commercial property hygiene and maintenance. In partnership with trusted contractors, we also undertake interior and exterior repair works, offering a complete solution for businesses that need more than just a surface clean.
